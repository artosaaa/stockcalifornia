# Stock California — homepage redesign mockup

## What this is
Static, framework-free redesign mockup of the homepage for StockCalifornia.com, a value toy retailer with two LA showrooms (Glendale, Tujunga). Built for approval before porting to Shopify OS 2.0 sections (see `README.md`). Includes:
- `index.html`: single-file homepage (announcement bar, hero, brand marquee, shop-by-age, searchable categories, best sellers with real Shopify CDN images/prices, showrooms, trust strip, newsletter, call FAB, purchase toasts, deal countdown, ride-on banner).
- 30 local SEO pages `toy-store-<city>.html` and 14 brand pages in `brands/`, plus `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`, all generated by `gen-seo.cjs`.

## Business / client
Stock California (stockcalifornia.com), toy store; Glendale showroom 6743 San Fernando Rd, (818) 511-5235; Tujunga showroom 7115 Foothill Blvd, (818) 205-5533. No folder in `/Users/a/Documents/Clients/`, so this looks like a prospect pitch / spec redesign rather than a signed client.

## Stack & how to run
- Plain HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, Google Fonts (Baloo 2 + Nunito). No package.json.
- Preview: open `index.html`, or `npx serve -l 4173 .` (`.claude/launch.json`).
- Regenerate SEO pages + sitemap/robots: `node gen-seo.cjs` (edit `CITIES`, brands and `SITE` at the top first).
- Deploy: Vercel static (`vercel.json`: `cleanUrls`, no trailing slash), presumably via Git integration on `main`.

## What's built and live
- Mockup live: https://stockcalifornia.vercel.app (HTTP 200, `server: Vercel`, title "Stock California — Toy Store in Glendale & Tujunga | LEGO, Barbie, Marvel"; verified 2026-11-17).
- The real https://stockcalifornia.com returns 200 but is the client's existing Shopify store behind Cloudflare, not this code.

## What looks unfinished
- Not ported to Shopify yet; that is the stated next step in `README.md` (plus 301s for misspelled collection handles, title length, prices on cards).
- `gen-seo.cjs:7` `SITE = 'https://stockcalifornia.vercel.app'`, so canonical URLs, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` all point at the vercel.app preview; swap to the real domain when ported, and consider noindex on the preview to avoid duplicate content.
- Purchase toasts (`index.html` ~line 894, `BUYS` array) show invented buyers ("Maria from Glendale just picked up...") with fake "2 min ago" times; this is fabricated social proof and should be replaced with real order data or removed before going live.
- Deal countdown (`tickTimer`, ~line 880) just counts to local midnight every day; it is artificial urgency, not tied to a real deal.
- Newsletter form (`index.html` ~line 838) only shows a thank-you and confetti; it submits nowhere.
- Add-to-cart buttons are a demo (bump the cart bubble only, ~line 860); no real cart.
- Unmerged branch `origin/backup/my-world`; check what it holds.
